## 🏆 Key Features & Architectural Highlights

### 1. 🎛️ Audio Experience (Web Audio API)
- Built-in, synthesized athletic UI micro-sounds (click tones, success chords, frequency transitions).
- Zero external audio files required — generates low-latency audio via the browser's native Web Audio API.
- Quick on/off toggle located directly in the header navigation.

### 2. ⚡ Live Atmosphere & Metrics Ticker
- Dynamic gym capacity and occupancy simulator (`64% Live Capacity`).
- Animated metric counters tracking facility specs, certified coaches, and member success rates.

### 3. 🏋️ Facility & Technology Showcase (5 Specialized Zones)
- Interactive tabs to switch through 5 specialized athletic zones:
  - **Zone 01**: Olympic Power & Heavy Iron Lab (Eleiko IPF calibrated platforms, monolifts)
  - **Zone 02**: Biometric Hyrox & Cardio Track (Concept2, Rogue Echo Bikes, 60m turf lane)
  - **Zone 03**: Recovery Sanctuary & Biohack Spa (Finnish 90°C cedar sauna, -110°C cryotherapy, 4°C cold plunge)
  - **Zone 04**: Titan Combat & Striking Arena (Championship ring, Fairtex teardrop bags)
  - **Zone 05**: Smart Refuel & Biohacking Lounge (InBody 970 diagnostics, peptide & amino bar)

### 4. 🎯 Biomechanical Muscle Targeter & Exercise Explorer
- Interactive body muscle group selector (Chest, Lats & Back, Deltoids, Arms, Legs & Glutes, Core).
- Dynamically displays:
  - Target exercise imagery & biomechanical classification
  - Coach execution cues and form tips
  - Recommended set/rep schemes
  - Titan Forge gym equipment inventory availability

### 5. 📅 Interactive Weekly Class Battle Schedule
- Filter sessions by **day of the week** (Monday through Sunday) and **discipline** (Heavy Strength, Hyrox Conditioning, Boxing Strike, Mobility & Yoga).
- Real-time remaining spot indicator with instant one-click booking confirmation and celebratory feedback.

### 6. 🧮 Athletic Biometrics Suite (3 Interactive Calculators)
- **Smart BMI & Body Composition Analyzer**: Calculates exact BMI, athletic category, optimal target weight range, and baseline BMR.
- **1-Rep Max (1RM) Estimator**: Uses the calibrated Brzycki formula to calculate your 1RM, plus 95%, 90%, 80%, and 70% rep-range load targets.
- **Daily Fuel & Macronutrient Breakdown**: Computes exact daily calories and grams of protein, carbs, and fats for Fat Shredding (Cut), Hypertrophy (Lean Bulk), or Maintenance.

### 7. 🤖 Instant AI-Style Workout Split Generator
- Select your primary focus (Hypertrophy, Raw Strength, Hyrox, Fat Loss), training days (3, 4, 5, or 6 days), and experience level.
- Instantly generates an exercise split with exercises, sets, reps, and RPE guidelines.
- Features a **1-click "Copy Split"** button to copy your custom routine to clipboard.

### 8. 📸 Before & After Interactive Transformation Slider
- Interactive split slider allowing users to slide left and right to compare real athletic transformations across 16-week cycles.

### 9. 💳 Transparent Membership Matrix
- Interactive **Monthly vs Annual (Save 25%)** billing switch.
- 3 Curated Tiers:
  - *Iron Access* ($69/mo)
  - *Elite Athlete* ($129/mo - "Most Popular")
  - *Titan Black VIP* ($249/mo - All-inclusive personal training & cryo spa)

### 10. 🎟️ VIP Pass Activation & Modals
- 3-Day Complimentary VIP Pass claiming system with form validation, confetti animation, and stylish toast notifications.
- 4K Facility Tour preview modal.
- Expandable FAQ accordion.
- Mobile responsive sliding navigation drawer.

## 🎨 Tech Stack & Design Philosophy

- **Framework**: Semantic HTML5 + Vanilla ES6+ Modular JavaScript
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS + Custom CSS (`styles.css`) with Onyx & Cyber Volt (`#CCFF00`) aesthetic
- **Icons**: Lucide Icons
- **Effects**: Canvas Confetti + Web Audio API Synthesizer
- **Typography**: Bebas Neue (Display Headers), Syne (Accents), Inter (Body), JetBrains Mono (Biometrics)
- **Zero build dependencies**: Works directly in any modern browser without requiring Node.js or npm.
